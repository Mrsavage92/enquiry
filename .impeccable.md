# Design Context

Compiled 2026-09-18 from the creator-authored research corpus (C:/Users/Adam/Documents/Enquiry/research: doc 02 ICP, doc 37 USP lock-in, doc 39 communication principles, doc 44 visual direction decision) and docs/CURRENT_PHASE.md. Not inferred from code.

## Users

Owner-run service businesses that quote before they book: painters, cleaners, mobile beauty, trades, wedding and event suppliers, creative and professional services. Australia first. The owner answers the customers, organises the work and makes the final call. They read between jobs on a laptop and check the phone at lunch. Customer messages arrive incomplete, ambiguous, or changed later; before replying the owner checks notes, calendar, website, price list, past messages or memory. Each good enquiry is economically meaningful; slow or leaked follow-up loses work. Existing CRM, FSM and booking tools are too heavy or solve only part of it (doc 02).

Job to be done: turn a messy customer request into a safe business decision before promising anything, then send a reply in their own voice.

## Brand Personality

Careful, plain-spoken, on the owner's side. Three words: honest, calm, specific. Emotional goal: relief and trust, never hype. "Unknown beats guessing." Nothing on screen implies activity that did not happen (doc 39 principle 7). Prepared is never sent.

Locked wedge (doc 37): know what you can safely promise before you reply. Yes. No. Not yet. Promise Check only asks for the detail that can change the answer.

## Aesthetic Direction

UI1 identity (Codex-owned): white working surfaces, soft lilac shell, violet accent #654ac2, Inter, sculptural ribbon hero. Doc 44 locked a hierarchy/density pass inside this identity, explicitly NOT a re-skin, new palette, new typeface, dark mode or hero rebuild. Light mode only. Warm paper/ink, hairline drawn-grid grammar on the home body (2026-09-18).

Anti-references: AI receptionist / "AI employee" category look; dark navy + gold; gradient text; glassmorphism; hero-metric layouts; uniform padded card grids; pointer-follow spotlights; stock photos of happy professionals.

## Design Principles

1. One decision, one screen, nothing competing for attention (doc 39 principle 2).
2. Show the real product, labelled as a sample workspace, never a mock-up presented as live.
3. Every claim on the page maps to shipped behaviour; no "only", "first", "never miss a lead", no automation claims.
4. Every text element measures at least 4.5:1 on its rendered background (3:1 large); secondary text never lighter than #68656d.
5. Motion only where it conveys a state change; prefers-reduced-motion respected everywhere.
