# Sitewide visual review - 15 September 2026

Owner request: extend the approved roadmap review, image exploration, critique and implementation approach across every site page. Allow creative contemporary visual exploration while preserving real product capabilities and safeguards.

Branch: `codex/ui1-sitewide-visual-review`, based on production `9ebff84`.

## Boundaries

Presentation, interaction clarity and truthful explanatory copy only. No auth bypass, tenant changes, commercial-engine rewrite, invented sending, integrations, analytics, billing, referral rewards or app-store availability. Previously accepted roadmap and entry aurora are baselines, not reasons to force every page into the same composition. Production website delivery follows the owner's standing instruction; beta and email-delivery gates remain separate.

## Review method

1. Inspect current source and actual rendered pages.
2. Ask original GPT conversation `6aa20b90-c690-83ec-af8b-b549fc7c2865` for independent product/design review.
3. Generate visual explorations by coherent page family, with real content and creative freedom.
4. Critique generated visuals; reject fabricated features or weaker usability.
5. Implement native, responsive interfaces, not images masquerading as functional UI.
6. Verify page coverage, states, keyboard use, layout and existing regression tests.

## Image critique and selection

Five boards are retained alongside the exact prompts. They are design evidence, not website product proof.

- `public-exploration.png`: borrow editorial rhythm and vertical storytelling. Reject art-gallery drift, handwriting, stock people and imaginary CRM UI.
- `workspace-exploration.png`: borrow agenda/date and row rhythm. Reject invented integrations, metrics, sending actions and changed mobile navigation.
- `entry-exploration.png`: borrow airy form rhythm and unsaved profile preview. Retain the accepted aurora; reject serif chrome and false confirmation claims.
- `proof-exploration.png`: borrow flat permission rows, timeline and legal contents. Reject replacement branding, fake article counts, billing and currency features.
- `gpt-refinement-board.png`: generated in the original GPT conversation after its source and screenshot review. Borrow How/Demo/Updates composition; reject fabricated release dates, time filters and the unsupported sending button.

No generated mockup is presented as an actual product screenshot. The Updates image is a capture of the existing production roadmap.

## Independent implementation critique

The original GPT chat reviewed six implemented desktop screenshots. It supported the How, Updates and Early Access direction, and identified three improvements: make the changed work window/scope explicit; prioritise Need your reply in Insights; constrain Settings/Support to 900-980px. All three were applied. A second review received the complete tablet story, revised Settings/Insights and the real mobile enquiry. Its verdict is recorded in the verification evidence.

The review was limited to the supplied screenshots and source. It is not independent sign-off of all product behavior, production email delivery, or beta readiness.
