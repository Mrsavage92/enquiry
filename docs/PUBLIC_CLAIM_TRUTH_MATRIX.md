# Classification

### SAFE NOW
Supported by current public/demo/product truth.

### SAFE AS EXPLICIT DEMO / VISION
May be shown as a clearly labelled demonstration/future product behaviour, but must not be presented as a live production integration/capability.

### NARROW BEFORE TRAFFIC
Current wording can reasonably imply a capability/evidence state that Enquiry has not earned yet.

### LATER / EVIDENCE REQUIRED
Do not claim until implementation or external evidence exists.

## `A customer writes in — form, text, Instagram, or mail.`

**Class:** NARROW OR EXPLICIT DEMO BEFORE TRAFFIC

Current production channel integrations are not all live.

The product model supports multi-channel provenance/continuity, but first beta may begin through manual/private paste.

Do not let a visitor reasonably conclude:

> connect Instagram/SMS/email today and Enquiry automatically ingests them

unless that is actually implemented.

Possible truthful forms:
- "Whether it came from a form, text, Instagram or email, bring the enquiry into one decision state."
- "Start with paste/forwarding; connected channels will roll out progressively."
- leave the fixture demo multi-channel example, but visibly label it as a product demonstration.

Exact copy belongs to public-traffic/9B review, not R2A.

---

## Video accessibility label: `A woman opens Enquiry on her phone. A new enquiry is already understood. She sends the reply.`

**Class:** SAFE ONLY IF "sends" is truthful to the demonstrated action

First-beta plan supports:
- owner reviews;
- copies/uses response externally;
- may manually record sent.

If the video visually shows Enquiry performing a real channel send while no integration exists, label/context should not imply current production send.

"You send" is a strong product message and can remain if the owner is clearly the actor.

# Integration-specific claims

Before deliberate public traffic, no page should imply production-live support for:

- Gmail/Microsoft mailbox sync;
- Instagram DMs;
- Facebook DMs;
- SMS;
- WhatsApp;
- calendar booking;
- payments;
- public customer quote/booking links.

unless the specific integration is genuinely deployed and tested.

### Allowed
- roadmap/future language;
- clearly labelled fixture/product demonstration;
- "supported channels will roll out";
- manual paste/forwarding capability when implemented.

---

# Automation claims

Do not claim:
- autonomous sending;
- autonomous decline;
- autonomous booking;
- "handles everything while you sleep."

The current trust model is stronger:

> decision → review/authority → action

Future earned autonomy must remain per action class.

---

# Customer/evidence claims

Before real external beta evidence, do not claim:

- customer counts;
- conversion increase;
- hours saved;
- booking lift;
- recommendation accuracy;
- customer testimonials;
- "trusted by";
- external service-business cohort collaboration.

Fixture/eval metrics are not customer results.

---

# Installability claims

Before Phase 10A passes:
- do not market "install the app";
- do not call Home Screen install production-ready;
- do not imply App Store/Play Store presence.

Existing PWA infrastructure is platform foundation, not product sign-off.
