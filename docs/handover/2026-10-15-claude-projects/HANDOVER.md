# Handover to Claude Projects - 2026-10-15

## What this is

A pre-launch, mobile-first app for owner-run Australian service businesses (painters, cleaners, mobile beauty), built for owners with ADHD. The owner pastes in a customer enquiry; the app works out what can safely be promised (Yes / No / Not yet), asks only for the detail that changes the answer, and prepares the reply. The owner still sends it. "Enquiry" is a working name.

Offer (locked by the founder, single source `src/lib/site/offer.ts`): founding members pay A$15 a month for as long as they stay, A$29 for anyone who joins after public launch, first-month refund, no cap. Payments are not open yet: the site collects an early-access list.

**Immediate goal:** share the site publicly for early sign-up (target Friday 2026-10-16).
